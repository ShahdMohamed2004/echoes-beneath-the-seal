Privacy policy

Effective 11 October 2026. Created and Designed by Shahd Mohamed.

The game has no accounts, advertising, cross-device save service, general tracking or remote AI. Progress, preferences, daily reports, ink marks and equipment are stored on this device. Exported saves are read only when selected. Clearing browser data can erase your progress.

Optional outcome statistics

No statistics request is made until you press a statistics button. “View statistics only” reads aggregate counts and does not submit your game. Sharing requires a separate unchecked consent box and your click. It sends an ending category, a small fixed set of Boolean outcomes (recorded choices, clues, infection and review accuracy) and a random receipt for this run. It sends no name, email, saved dialogue or full save file. The random receipt is used only for idempotency; it is not an account or permanent device ID. Statistics measure opted-in submitted runs, not verified unique people. The server cannot prove that a client actually played a run.

Supabase may process connection metadata such as IP addresses. The game service retains a daily hash of the gateway-reported address with count buckets to impose best-effort limits of 3 submissions per minute and 20 per day. It does not retain the raw address in the game tables. Hashing is not a guarantee of anonymity. Receipts and outcome rows remain on the service; there is no self-service withdrawal from the game. Shared networks may share limits. See Supabase privacy.

The older version’s opt-in ending totals remain separate from the detailed outcome sample. Old consent is never silently reused for additional data. Missing historical decisions are excluded from their applicable denominators. Samples smaller than five are labeled insufficient, with actual counts displayed.

Hosting and assets

GitHub Pages hosts the web edition and may log network requests under its own policy. Art, fonts, and sound recordings are packaged locally with their licenses; no external asset CDNs are used at runtime. The offline web cache stores public game files, never statistics responses. Native wrappers consume the same build but their stores and permissions may have platform-specific behavior. No camera, microphone, contacts or location access is requested.

سياسة الخصوصية

اللعبة بلا حسابات أو إعلانات أو مزامنة حفظ. التقدم والإعدادات وتقارير الوردية وأثر الحبر محفوظة محليًا. مسح بيانات المتصفح قد يمسحها؛ صدّر نسخة أولًا.

الإحصائيات اختيارية. عرض المجاميع وحده لا يرسل نتيجتك. المشاركة تتطلب موافقة منفصلة، وترسل فئة النهاية ونتائج مختصرة وإيصالًا عشوائيًا للحكاية لمنع تكرار الإرسال، دون أسماء أو بريد أو ملف حفظ كامل. قد تعالج Supabase عنوان الاتصال. نخزن ملخصًا يوميًا للعنوان كما يصل للخدمة لتقييد الطلبات، دون العنوان الخام في جداول اللعبة. التجزئة لا تضمن إخفاء الهوية. لا توجد طريقة داخل اللعبة لسحب مشاركة مسجلة، والأرقام لا تثبت عدد لاعبين فريدين أو أن كل حكاية لُعبت فعلًا.

موافقة النسخة القديمة لا تُستخدم تلقائيًا للبيانات الجديدة. عدد المشاركات المؤهلة ظاهر لكل نسبة، والعينة الأقل من خمس مشاركات تُوصف بأنها غير كافية.
