# World and character bible

Created and Designed by Shahd Mohamed

The setting is an unnamed contemporary Egyptian administrative district. Its visual language is municipal tile, steel shelving, worn brass, old paper and blue-green institutional paint. Do not introduce unrelated mythology. The Echo is the residue of neural/memory experiments and material traces, not a randomly selected demon.

## Location atlas

```text
Residential blocks —— Registry Window 4 —— Hospital road —— Emergency ward
                          │                                     │
                     Basement archive —— Restricted corridor —— Echo annex
```

The office is the fixed investigation view. Street, corridor, archive, hospital, laboratory and memory reconstruction are shown through films and evidence; they are not freely walkable maps. Registry → hospital is the documented transfer route. The basement archive shares the annex access system. Exact travel times are not asserted.

## Chronology

Three years before the present: the clerk volunteers for a therapeutic trial and authenticates transfer of fourteen participants. The institution changes the procedure and conceals its consequences. Dr. Salim Mourad administers memory erasure; repeated conditioning and reassignment return the clerk to the registry. A 2024 leak and concealment are retained from the original Echo record. The current fifteen-shift crisis reveals the difference between the initial trial, the later leak, and the present outbreak. Paper preserves pressure/ink layers, explaining why manipulated digital records do not erase every trace. Echo manifestations remain ambiguous perceptions; the original volunteer history is not rewritten as a dream.

## Palette and motion

Deep ink #101820, shadow teal #26454b, paper #e4d5ad, amber #e8ac65 and pale light #ffe3aa. Skin and clothing use controlled ramps. Ink/shadow discontinuity identifies Echo effects; ordinary dust/rain never use that motif. Animation changes hands, face, shoulders and walk stride; transformations alter eyes, markings and silhouette without arbitrary horns.

## World-state checklist

- Early registry: queue, functioning machinery and clear street; ordinary clerical anomalies teach comparison.
- Day 3 onward: rain appears with the rain sound layer.
- Day 6 onward: door interactions can barricade; CCTV trace becomes available.
- Day 7 onward: street witnesses appear; ambulance choice adds a vehicle representation.
- Day 9 onward: a visible window crack and restricted world cues reflect crisis progression.
- Late darkness: actual active sound sources drive threat; light/barricade give a visible response.
- Open drawer, archive, window, lights, barricade, props, printed copies and discoveries persist in saves.

These scenes convey a connected district; this release does not implement free-roaming geography, pathfinding, destructible architecture or fully simulated background citizens.
