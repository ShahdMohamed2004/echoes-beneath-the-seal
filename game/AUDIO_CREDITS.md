# Audio credits

12 recordings were acquired from Kenney's Impact Sounds pack on 2026-10-10 and integrated locally. Creator: Kenney. Individual pack page: https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds . License stated on that page and in the acquired archive: CC0. The archive's exact license is retained in licenses/Kenney-Impact-Sounds.txt. The manifest records original source, local path and use. The recordings were converted to mono 22050 Hz PCM WAV with FFmpeg to use a common browser format, including older iOS WebViews.

Mappings: impactWood_medium → wood; impactMetal_light → metal; impactSoft_medium → paper/contact; footstep_concrete → step; impactGlass_light → glass; impactWood_heavy → stamp. Variants 000 and 001 are supplied for each. Soft impacts approximate paper contact; they are not described as recordings of paper rustling.

Seven WAV assets (rain, hum, echo, printer, phone, breath, music) are original seeded-noise and harmonic sound design generated by tools/art.py. They are synthesized beds and cues, not field recordings or human performances. Copyright © 2026 Shahd Mohamed. All rights reserved for these original compositions, subject to applicable law.

The original 19 local assets are loaded by src/audio.js; the 3 recorded layers, 8 CC0 environment effects and 6 nonverbal human recordings listed below bring the current total to 36. Playback uses category gain, contextual loops, stereo positioning, door-dependent low-pass filtering, impact thresholds and per-event cooldowns. Important cues have text alternatives. Assets are listed in audio-manifest.json and included in the production offline cache. No proprietary audio was taken from the visual-reference games.

Selected files are trimmed to at most 1–8 seconds, converted to mono 16000 Hz PCM WAV and peak-limited with FFmpeg. These are attributed sound effects; their acquisition is not a claim that every sample is an unprocessed field recording. Crossfaded local buffers, positional panning, distance attenuation, low-pass occlusion and an original algorithmic room impulse operate at runtime. The seven mixer categories persist with the game save. Six human nonverbal cues are recorded; full spoken dialogue is not voiced. Dialogue remains bilingual text, complemented by contextual breath, cough, gasp, laughter, hush, creature cues and material gestures.

## Recorded human reactions

Six nonverbal recordings by **OwlishMedia**, from https://opengameart.org/content/sound-effects-pack (source page identifies CC0 1.0, verified 2026-10-11). Direct pack: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/Owlish%20Media%20Sound%20Effects.zip . Original paths: `Human/breath-male.wav`, `Human/breath-female.wav`, `Human/cough1.wav`, `Human/gasp1.wav`, `Human/laugh1.wav`, `Human/shh1.wav`. Only short selected excerpts are shipped; leading silence is removed, peaks limited and files converted to mono 16000Hz PCM. Individual source paths and hashes are in the manifest; provenance is in `licenses/OwlishMedia-CC0.txt`. Small contextual rate differences distinguish profiles. These are licensed nonverbal samples, not cloned voices or full Egyptian dialogue performances.

No autoplay restriction is bypassed: the game attempts ambience and provides an Enable world sound button if the browser requires a gesture. Essential clues have text/visual equivalents and remain accessible while muted.
